Group

Project Revive was a project shared between 6 individuals. The group shared their time, resources, and specialities to create a Pterodactyl artefact.

There were 6 individuals consisting of Adriano, Taran, MJ, Tom, Dami and Ganesh

Taran

Taran was the main designer; he created both the prototype and final model designs. He created the designs using Blender because of his familiarity with the software. He also took on the role of team leader, ensuring that all members of the group knew their roles ensuring that everything was moving efficiently.

MJ

Mary was the scribe; she ensured that whenever someone had something completed, it would be documented to keep track of where the group were and to see which options were viable for the project so that they could make a successful final product.

Tom

Tom was looking into the type of material the 3D print should be made out of, this is because of his previous experience with handling 3D printers/prints. Allowing us to create a model that is suitable for its intended environment.

Dami

Dami has been using University Resources to identify what departments within the University would be of use to develop the artefact. He was also in charge of identifying different 'skins' so that the Pterodactyl could be more realistic.

Adriano

Adriano was in charge of communication; he used the information obtained by his teammates to talk to the individuals so that the model could be developed. He was also in charge of creating the Risk assessment chart so that they were able to understand the possible risks and know how to avoid them appropriately, and so that injuries could be minimised.

Ganesh

Ganesh was in charge of finding ways to make the pterodactyl flap its wings through multiple methods, including motors and strings. Originally, the motor was his main path, but then, with the restrictions of the budget, we resorted to using strings to make it move.
