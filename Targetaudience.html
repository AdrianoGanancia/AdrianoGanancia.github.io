Target Audience

Who is this for?

Project Revival is intended for primary schools.

Why?

Children have had an increasing exposure to virtual content, this online content can affect the way a childs cognitive function which has impacted their cognitive function, and emotion regulation by 12% (George, Larson and Sam, 2025).

This is where Project Revive comes into play.

Having an interactable model allows for children to take a look away from the whiteboard, and look towards being able to play around with a dinosaur. We collectively chose to Pterodactyl because of children's key interest in dinosaurs this will ensure that theyll be engaged, while being fun and engaging it can also be educating being able to be used in lessons regarding history.
