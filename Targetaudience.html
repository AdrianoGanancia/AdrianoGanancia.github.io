Target Audience

Who is this for?

Project Revival is intended for primary schools.

Why?

Children have had an increasing exposure to virtual content, this online content has impacted their cognitive function, and emotion regulation by 12% (George, Larson and Sam, 2025).

This is where Project Revival comes into play.

Having an interactable model allows for children to take a look away from the whiteboard, and look towards being able to play around with a dinosaur. As a group it was collectively chosen to Pterodactyl because of children's key interest in dinosaurs this will ensure that theyll be engaged, while having fun because of the strings providing a source for the kids to interact with the model and teachers alike, and it can also be educating being able to be used in lessons regarding history specifically when talking about the Jurrsaic period. These topics can stretch throughout Key Stage 2 in the English education system meaning that it probably won't just be locked behind a cupboard collecting dust or in a landfill.


If there was more time then a development would be to use a Haptic Pen to implement some texture into the artefact.

What role does texture have?

Adding texture into the artefact can provide sensory aid which can be soothing to children with sensory needs, however it is also important to consider children that are Hyper-sensitive to certain textures which is common amongst children with Aspergers (National Autistic Society, n.d.) this is why the current artefact doesnt have any texturing.

How to store it?

As classrooms can get relatively hot with ~30 student per class and a teacher, in warmer seasons like summer the plastic could get quite hot which is why it should be contained within a case to prevent any children from accidentally touching it on a hot summer day. Another reason for the security feature of having a case is to prevent the children from throwing it around because if it was bare open then something could break off which isnt ideal.
